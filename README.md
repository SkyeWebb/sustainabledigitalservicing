Sustainable Digital Servicing Application 
Where customers can get a ‘Sustainability / Green Score’ based on how they manage their communications preferences, with recommendations on how to improve / go greener!

A reusable API that lets customers:-
•	manage how they receive communications and documents, 
•	calculates an illustrative score based on their digital choices, and
•	recommends eligible changes that could increase the score

What problem are we trying to solve:

Customer may receive multiple documents – statements, policy documents, tax statements and general correspondence through a mixture of paper and digital channels.  Preferences can be difficult to understand or change, and the customer may not have a clear view of how extensively they use digital servicing.

The Group wants to increase the adoption of the mobile applications available (W&I are targeting a specific number of Scottish Widows app registrations in 2026). This type of functionality / service could contribute to customer adoption via green/sustainability incentives.

Proposed solution:

Build a REST API that stores communication and document preferences, calculates a Sustainability Servicing Score and returns recommendations on how to improve.
The UI could show a chart / lever for this and allow a customer to change preferences for the accounts/documents they have ability to update.
